# Bad guys (and the apprentice)

## The wizard

- The bad guys Jon heard about on the iPad: they only heard about the ping-pong guy and the witch. They don't learn the wizard is bad until he reveals it later (in the castle atrium).

- Looks (08-wizard.png): flat purple hat, curly grey hair, big nose, smile, long grey beard, huge purple robe with wide jagged sleeves.
- He's from Los Angeles, where he was already pretty sneaky.
- When he hears the kids are coming to Manor Konsz, he goes there too. Now he's even more sneaky, and he's ready to defeat the kids.
- The kids are trying to defeat the wizard.

## The witch

- Another bad guy. (Looks: see 06-witch.png.)
- She's on the bad guy team: all of the bad guys work as a team together (with the wizard).
- She lives in a swamp near the entrance of the wizard castle.
- She only drinks tea (not coffee).
- She has a wand. She secretly tries to trap the kids in her swamp with it.
- The witch's ministry: "The Witch's Ministry LLC" is her business operations for building capital to make a giant witch's castle on chicken legs. Why the wizard would join: he has a secret crush on her and wants her to be his girlfriend. But she doesn't like him very much and is always mean to him.

## The ping-pong bad guy

- A ping-pong bad guy drinking beer in his left hand (07-ping-pong-bad-guy.png). On the bad guy team.
- He hits people with his ping-pong stick, and he drinks beer.
- The kids meet him at the creepy ping-pong station, in the middle of a mountain.

## The apprentice

- Drawn at the witch's house in the swamp, in a boat because the swamp has water in it (10-witchs-house.png).
- He starts as bad, but he'll become good.
- Eventually, in the story, he finds sympathy with the kids and aligns more with them.
- When the apprentice turns good: after they fight the wizard's dogs, and the wizard joins the witch in the house (which moves around the map), the apprentice is in the swamp. If they give him the right compliment (3 choices), he will join them. The compliment choices (from Story Keeper's ideas; the kids said to use them all as the choices, and #3 is the winner): "You're a really good rower!" / "You'd make a better wizard than the wizard." / **"You're too nice to be on the bad guy team."** (the right one) / "Your pointy hat is super cool." (So there are 4 choices now, not 3.)

## The dogs of darkness

- 3 vicious hounds. The wizard sets them on the kids in the castle atrium, then vanishes to join the witch in her house ([zones/09-wizard-castle.md](zones/09-wizard-castle.md)).
- **How it ends with the wizard and the witch** (2026-11-08): at the top of the mansion, the kids, wearing the witch's ministry robes, offer to marry them. They are happy and stop fighting the kids. (See [zones/secret-mansion.md](zones/secret-mansion.md).)
- Why they were bad (2026-11-08): the wizard and witch were just grumpy because they were lonely. Now they are happy, and the world is going to be more pleasant.
- At the end, the wizard, the witch, and the apprentice help fix the kids' ship. The apprentice joins the kids in the search for the chicken house and gives the hint about the marriage.
