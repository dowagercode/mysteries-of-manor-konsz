# 3. The ping-pong mountain

## Place

- **The creepy ping-pong station** — in the middle of a mountain. The ping-pong bad guy is there.

- **Buffer scenes** (Dandy, 2026-11-08): a scene of the kids **going up the mountain** before the ping-pong station, and one of them **going down the mountain** after the fight (see [../gameplay.md](../gameplay.md)).
- **Debug** (Dandy, 2026-11-08): the debug menu should be able to jump to the mountain **after the ping-pong guy is defeated**.

## Story

- After they swim, they run to a mountain, and then they meet the ping-pong bad guy (at the creepy ping-pong station in the middle of the mountain).
- The ping-pong bad guy keeps trying to hit them, but he can't, because when Olivia spins around, all of her friends keep on floating, and he can't hit them.
- The floating kids are getting tired of the fight, so they take his ping-pong stick and hit him with it themselves.
- He is knocked out, so they can get away now.

## How the fight plays

- Ping-pong scene: you can click on each character and have them do their special move. The special move should not happen by default when you select a character (you choose to use it). Special moves in the ping-pong fight: Olivia spins and floats her friends. Jasin kicks the soccer ball, and it glows and can be used as a fireball. Jon has his portal, so he can hide everyone inside to protect them. May has telekinesis and can throw objects or people around.
- How they beat the ping-pong bad guy (kids started from Plan 3, "Team Combo," from options I offered, then changed the order): 1. The ping-pong guy attacks the first time → Jon hides everyone in his portal. 2. Before Olivia comes out → Jasin kicks his fireball. 3. The ping-pong guy attacks again → Olivia spins and lifts everyone up, and May grabs the stick with her mind and bonks him. He's knocked out.

## Items and puzzles

- **Ping-pong stick** — the ping-pong bad guy's paddle → the kids take it and hit him with it → he's knocked out and they get away.
