# 9. The wizard's castle

(The wizard himself is described in [../bad-guys.md](../bad-guys.md). The priest disguises come from the swamp: see [08-swamp-and-witch.md](08-swamp-and-witch.md).)

## Place

- **The secret castle** — separate from the mansion, 50 miles away but still in Manor Konsz. The wizard is there. You get there in a creepy jeep. Looks (09-wizard-castle.png, "Wizard castle"): twisty claw-like towers, flame-shaped spires, a purple flag on the tallest tower, a round window with a cross, lots of small windows, an arched door with a winding path, grey sky, crescent moon.
- **The truck entrance** — behind the castle (first a game developer idea; the kids said keep it for now). It goes to the **kitchen**, which connects to the **main reception area where they have tea** (where the wizard has the snack with the disguised kids).
- Arriving: a very vast, barren landscape up in the mountain, with a lot of distance between where you can drive to and the castle. A gigantic front door that's closed and very far away. Very dangerous rocks to climb to get to the door.

## Story

- When they arrive at the castle, it's a very vast, barren landscape up in the mountain. There's a lot of space and distance between where they can drive to and the castle. It has a gigantic front door, but it's closed and very far away. They have to go through very dangerous rocks to climb to the door.
- The kids must get through the rocks by understanding the correct sequence of moves. There will be a kind of pattern, but it will not be apparent at first. They have to mimic a pattern in the clouds, which keeps on changing for what's needed.
- At the gigantic front door: they disguise themselves as priests from the witch's ministry and are trying to convince the wizard to join them.
- When the disguised kids come to the door, the wizard says: "Finally, she noticed me!" (He has a secret crush on the witch: see [../bad-guys.md](../bad-guys.md).) He invites them in for lemonade and muffins. The kids will be having a polite conversation with the wizard. They are asking about the strange glowing. He's about to tell them, but then he asks a question about the witch, which they don't know the answer to, and then he disappears. His question: "What is her favorite way to drink coffee?" (She only drinks tea.) After he disappears, there is a **spiral staircase** that goes up to the level with the wheel-of-the-year hall.

## Items and puzzles

- **Dangerous rocks puzzle** — at the wizard castle, between where they park and the gigantic front door → get through by understanding the correct sequence of moves. There's a kind of pattern, but it's not apparent at first. They figure it out by mimicking a pattern in the clouds, which keeps on changing for what's needed.
- **The maze inside the castle** — a riddle from the stone statue in the field by the swamp (once they put its head back on) helps the kids navigate it ([08-swamp-and-witch.md](08-swamp-and-witch.md)). The riddle: "Follow the wheel of the year." There will be a hall of 8 rooms, each one with a name based on the Celtic wheel of the year. They will not be in order, but must be entered in that order. There will also be a chart with the wheel of the year in one of the rooms, as a hint. Where the chart is: there will be a closet in the Samhain room, and the chart will be in it, under a cloth. If they go in the wrong order: nothing. But they need to enter in the correct order to open a staircase that will rise in the center room. The staircase goes up to the next level, which will have another series of puzzles to solve.
  - For reference (from Story Keeper, not the kids): the eight festivals of the wheel of the year, in order, are Samhain, Yule, Imbolc, Ostara, Beltane, Litha, Lughnasadh, and Mabon.
- **The castle's next level** — up the staircase from the center room of the wheel-of-the-year hall. Another series of puzzles to solve. Who's there: a **sphinx** that they must pass. The sphinx will ask a riddle. The answer to the riddle is placed across 4 of the rooms on the lower level: the rooms for Yule, Ostara, Litha (kids said "Lita"), and Mabon. The riddle (the kids picked option 1 of the riddles Story Keeper suggested when they asked for ideas): "I'm buried in the ground, but I'm not treasure. Give me water and sun, and I'll grow." Answer: **SEED**, with one letter hidden in each of the 4 rooms. (Story Keeper's suggestion, for the kids to confirm: the letters go in wheel order, so S in Yule, E in Ostara, E in Litha, D in Mabon.) When they answer, the sphinx lets them pass into the next room, which is a hall of mirrors.
- **The hall of mirrors** — the next room after the sphinx, on the castle's upper level. They see a lot of reflections of themselves. Hidden in the hall of mirrors are three different rooms: **past**, **present**, and **future**.
  - **The past room** — when they go into the past room, they all turn into child versions of themselves, even smaller, like little babies that can walk around: toddlers. They're in a room that has giant furniture. The problem: there is a cookie on top of the table, but it is very high and they can't reach it. The only way they can get to it is if they move a chair so that they can climb up, but they also need to find a book to stand on to get to the top of the chair. There are also other objects in the room to distract them from this. When they eat the cookie, they get one third of a **crystal horn** (like a trumpet).
  - **The present room** — they will be their normal size, but in a fog. They need a flashlight to break through the fog, but that is found on the ship, in the armory ([01-spaceship.md](01-spaceship.md)). Through the fog, they find a giant tree, with an owl. The owl asks another riddle, which they must answer. The owl's riddle (the kids picked option 4 of the riddles Story Keeper suggested when they asked for ideas): "The more you take, the more you leave behind. What am I?" Answer: **footsteps**. When they answer, the owl gives them the middle section of the trumpet, and they go back to the main hall of mirrors.
  - Crystal horn pieces: the kids said "the reward is a mouthpiece of the crystal trumpet," and then, about the present room, "the reward is the middle section of the trumpet." Present room → the **middle section** (confirmed: the owl gives it to them). Past room (cookie) → the **mouthpiece** (Story Keeper's reading; kids to confirm).
  - **The future room** — it will be dark, and they need the flashlight to guide them in the dark again. It will be full of statues of the kids as adults. They must find the items made of stone that represent each kid and put them on the correct adult statue to get the **bell** of the crystal trumpet. Stone items: May has a hair bow. Jon has a portal disk. Jasin has a soccer ball. Olivia has a tutu. (The kids first said Jon = soccer ball and Jasin = portal disk, then switched them to match the characters.)
- **Cookie on the table (past room)** — too high for the toddler kids → move a chair to climb up → find a book to stand on to get to the top of the chair → reach the cookie → eat it → get one third of a crystal horn (like a trumpet). Other objects in the room are there to distract them.
- **Stone items and adult statues (future room)** — dark room, use the flashlight → find the stone items that represent each kid → put each on the correct adult statue of that kid → get the bell of the crystal trumpet. Items: May — hair bow; Jon — portal disk; Jasin — soccer ball; Olivia — tutu.
- **The crystal trumpet** — put together from the mouthpiece (past room), the middle section (present room, from the owl), and the bell (future room) → when they blow it, the hall of mirrors shatters, and they are in a large atrium with the wizard laughing at them.
- **The atrium** — a large atrium, revealed when the hall of mirrors shatters. The wizard is there, laughing at them. Before the wizard sends the dogs of darkness, the kids will approach the wizard and try to ask about the glowing mysteries. The wizard says that the things are glowing because they should give it to him. When the kids refuse, he will start the fight. The wizard says: "You little twerps, you think you can fight us? Try to defeat these dogs of darkness." And then the wizard vanishes, to join the witch in her house ([08-swamp-and-witch.md](08-swamp-and-witch.md)). The **dogs of darkness** will be 3 vicious hounds. (How the kids beat them: asking the kids.)
