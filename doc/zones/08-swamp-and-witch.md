# 8. The swamp and the witch

Third stop on the road map. (The witch herself is described in [../bad-guys.md](../bad-guys.md).)

## Place

- The witch's swamp: near the entrance of the wizard castle. The witch lives there. Her house stands on big bird legs in the swamp, with a little tower and a smoking chimney (10-witchs-house.png).

- **The field on the edge of the swamp** — a field with lots of grass, and there's straw, and there are some little frogs. There's also a stone statue of a guy on a horse, but his head is gone, and the horse looks like it's got its front legs in the air. The statue's head is in another part of the forest, stuck in a hole in a big tree ([07-scary-forest.md](07-scary-forest.md)). When they put the head on the statue, it will turn to them and speak. CHANGED: the secret it tells them isn't about the way into the castle; instead, it's a riddle that helps them navigate the maze inside the castle ([09-wizard-castle.md](09-wizard-castle.md)). (Earlier idea, now replaced: it told them a secret way in, around behind at the truck entrance.) The riddle: "Follow the wheel of the year."

## Story

- Next, they go to find the witch.
- When the kids walk away, the witch secretly, so secret, uses her wand. She waves it and tries to get the kids back over and trap them in her swamp. But when Jasin hears it, he kicks his soccer ball right into the witch's guts.
- Jasin kicked so hard, the witch fell down.
- They take her to the house and lock her in a cage.
- They get into the jeep and drive to the castle.

## How it plays

- Witch scene: no single kid. Where you point and click, the whole group moves together.

## Items and puzzles

- **Priest disguises** — the kids disguise themselves as priests from the witch's ministry → at the castle's gigantic front door → to try to convince the wizard to join them. They get the disguises from exploring around the witch's house. The witch's ministry: "The Witch's Ministry LLC" is her business operations for building capital to make a giant witch's castle on chicken legs. Why the wizard would join: he has a secret crush on her and wants her to be his girlfriend. But she doesn't like him very much and is always mean to him.
- **The apprentice's boat** — the kids go to the swamp on foot and steal the boat from the witch's apprentice → they use it to get back to the ship in the water for the space flower ([01-spaceship.md](01-spaceship.md), [07-scary-forest.md](07-scary-forest.md)). How they steal it: they have to create a distraction by making a giant scarecrow using twigs, and also an old shirt that they find in the forest, a straw hat, and some straw that they can stuff things in. Then they hide and watch the apprentice see the figure and run away as it looms in the fog.
- **Giant scarecrow** — made from twigs, an old shirt (found in the forest), a straw hat, and some straw to stuff it → set up as a distraction at the swamp → the apprentice sees it looming in the fog and runs away → the kids can take his boat. Twigs: a bundle next to a tree in the forest. Straw hat: underneath the rubble of the store, when the lady is freed from being under there. Straw: in the field on the edge of the swamp.
- **Fixing the statue** — put the statue head (from the hole in the big tree in the forest) back on the headless statue in the field → the statue turns to the kids and speaks → it tells them a riddle, "Follow the wheel of the year," that helps them navigate the maze inside the wizard's castle ([09-wizard-castle.md](09-wizard-castle.md)).
- Later (from the castle atrium): the wizard vanishes to join the witch in her house ([09-wizard-castle.md](09-wizard-castle.md)). **He lets the witch out of the cage.** This is shown in a **cutscene**: the wizard goes to the witch and frees her, then the house starts moving, and then it goes back to the kids. Then they go mobile with the house (it moves around the map), and the kids need to find it with clues. **The clues:** they will find **chicken feathers** in the place where the house was last. The place where it will be next will have **wind blowing**. They have to go out to the map and then back in to see it. The house goes to **all the locations on the map**. They must **catch the house 3 times** this way to finally be able to get to it. The first and second time they catch it, it **runs away and leaves feathers**. The third time, it ends at the **secret mansion's exterior**: the kids climb inside the house, but the wizard and the witch run out into the mansion (see [secret-mansion.md](secret-mansion.md)).
- **The apprentice joins the kids** — later, after the dogs of darkness: the witch's house (with the wizard and the witch in it) moves around the map. The apprentice is in the swamp. Give him the right compliment (out of 3 choices) and he will join them. The compliment choices (from Story Keeper's ideas; the kids said to use them all as the choices, and #3 is the winner): "You're a really good rower!" / "You'd make a better wizard than the wizard." / **"You're too nice to be on the bad guy team."** (the right one) / "Your pointy hat is super cool." (So there are 4 choices now, not 3.) **How he helps** (2026-11-08): the apprentice joins the kids in the search for the chicken house (the witch's moving house), and he gives the hint about the marriage (offering to marry the wizard and the witch, see [secret-mansion.md](secret-mansion.md)).
