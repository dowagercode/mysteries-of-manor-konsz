# 7. The scary forest

Second stop on the road map.

## Place

- **Zone: the scary forest** — second stop on the road map, after the supermarket. Who's there: different pairs of eyes. If you click on them, it reveals different creatures. Some of them are just like raccoons and possums and things like that. One specific one won't be available to click right away, but will appear after some time: a big, giant tree monster whose name is **Woody**. The problem: some trees are blocking the road, so they can't drive the jeep. What fixes it: they need to ask Woody to help move the trees, and then the trees will stand up and walk away. They were just sleeping. What it takes to get Woody to help: they must bring him a **space flower**, which is on the windowsill in the kitchen of the ship. They will go back to the ship unless they were really smart to grab it at the beginning. They will go back to the ship because Woody says to them, "I wish I had a space flower," and one of them will remember something about having had a space flower somewhere on the ship, and they'll go back and look. The kid who remembers is **Jasin**. To get back to the ship (it's in the water), they have to steal the boat from the apprentice of the witch, by going to the swamp on foot.

## Items and puzzles

- **Trees blocking the road** — scary forest → the trees are just sleeping → bring Woody (the giant tree monster) the space flower from the windowsill in the ship's kitchen → he helps → the trees stand up and walk away, and the jeep can drive on.
- **Old shirt** — found in the forest → used to make the giant scarecrow at the swamp ([08-swamp-and-witch.md](08-swamp-and-witch.md)).
- **Bundle of twigs** — next to a tree in the forest → used to make the giant scarecrow at the swamp ([08-swamp-and-witch.md](08-swamp-and-witch.md)).
- **Statue head** — in another part of the forest, stuck in a hole in a big tree → belongs to the headless stone statue (a guy on a horse) in the field on the edge of the swamp ([08-swamp-and-witch.md](08-swamp-and-witch.md)).
