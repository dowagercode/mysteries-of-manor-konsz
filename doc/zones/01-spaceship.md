# 1. The spaceship

Scene 1. You play as May.

## Story and how it plays

- Scene 1 (spaceship): you play as May. She has to figure out how to get Jon out (of his hideout). There will be a little mystery around that, with some objects nearby that she can use to entice him out (objects not decided yet). May has to go and talk to some of the other characters to find out exactly what Jon needs to come out, because he's actually trying to hide from someone. Jon knows who, but he's not going to tell anyone. In his mind he's saying, "It's the bad guys." He heard on the iPad in the room that there are bad guys, so he's hiding in a secret room.
- Scene 1 rules: the game should not move to the next part of the story until the first part is resolved. May goes around the ship and asks other crew members what's going on. The whole ship can be explored, with rooms inside to explore (Crew: Olivia, May, Jon, and Jasin.)
- Spaceship rooms: 4 bedrooms, one kitchen, one living room, one bathroom, one playroom, the cockpit ("it's a spaceship, so they need the cockpit"), a control room area, an engine room, and storage. Each room needs a door, and there's a hallway that connects them all. Jon's hideout is in Jon's room: it's a portable hole, so it doesn't need its own door in the hallway. May can go inside it too, to talk to him.
- Ship layout (Dandy): the hallway is in the center of all the rooms, like a real ship, with the cabins on the sides of a central hallway. The engineering room is at the rear end, and the control room is at the fore (front) end. The kitchen is its own kind of zone; maybe the kitchen and the rec room are connected to each other in the center, like the hub of everything. Confirmed by Dandy: the living room is the rec room, and the control room and cockpit are one room. Order along the hallway, back to front: engine room, storage, bathroom, May's room, Jon's room, the hub (kitchen + living room joined by an open archway), Jasin's room, Olivia's room, playroom, control room.
- Ship look (Dandy): the ship looks too homogeneous right now. Each room needs its own distinctive decoration and look. Don't make it a monotonous hallway; maybe view it from a different angle. Exploring should be fun and interesting, not a chore. (What each room looks like: ask the kids.) May's room: see 12-mays-room.png (hearts, heart balloons, Barbie Dream House). Jasin's room: see 13-jasins-room.png (soccer balls everywhere).
- Ship view (Dandy picked, in the game developer session): a cutaway, like a dollhouse sliced open from the side. It replaces the long hallway and the map. Engine room at the back (left), control room at the front (right). Top deck: bathroom, Olivia's room, playroom. Middle deck: kitchen + living room hub, Jasin's room. Bottom deck: May's room, Jon's room, storage. May climbs ladders between decks. Room decorations are placeholders until the kids describe or draw their rooms.
- Clue style (picked from options I offered): Dollhouse View + Follow the Mouse. The kitchen mouse peeks out in different rooms, leaving little footprints or crumbs that lead toward the next clue, and kids follow the trail around the ship.
- Camera and exits (Dandy, after trying it): show the exit on screen. Move away from the side view. Have doors on both sides. Use more of a cinematic angle that shifts with the different locations you go to.
- UPDATE (Dandy, in the game developer session): the ship now uses cinematic hallway shots — an aft hallway toward the engine room and a fore hallway toward the control room, with doors on both sides — connected by the kitchen + living room hub. The dollhouse view is saved for the wizard's castle.
- When May asks Olivia what's going on with Jon, Olivia says: "I don't know. I can't tell you unless you bring me my memory pills."
- To find Olivia's memory pills, you have to go into the engine room, but the key to the engine room is inside the refrigerator, in a can of tuna.
- The spaceship is stalled in space and not moving. May needs Jon to help, because he's the engineer. (That's why she needs the engine room map.)
- When May brings Olivia her memory pills, Olivia remembers that Jon likes chocolate cake a lot. So they have to make a chocolate cake for Jon to get him to come out of the hole.
- May uses the chocolate cake to convince Jon to come out and fix the ship.
- To fix the ship, Jon needs the instructions the mouse has (the engine room schematics). He uses them to repair the engine, which is offline.
- After Jon fixes the engine, they get hit by asteroids (which make the spaceship fall into the water).
- Control room (from Dandy and the kids, told to the game developer session): see 20-control-room.png. Big window showing a sun, a striped planet, and a satellite; a control panel with colorful buttons; a door that says "Armory: do not enter unless you have keycard," with a red keycard reader.
- The armory (behind the door in the control room) has all kinds of weird space weapons in it. Looks: see 22-armory.png (weapons in display cases on the wall). Names and what each weapon does: not decided yet.
- Hallway doors (Dandy, told to the game developer session, about 21-hallway-doors.png): the doors won't all be right next to each other. They'll be spaced out along a hallway, with doors on both sides, the engine room at one end, and the control room at the other end. The drawing shows the doors and their colors (each kid's door is their room's color), not the exact spacing. Which doors go on which side, and in what order: still open.
- Kitchen + rec room block (Dandy, told to the game developer session): the rec room (living room) and the kitchen stay together as their own block, like the kitchen + living room hub the game already has. The hallway with the different cabins is separate from that block.

- Room look (Dandy, after trying the game): the rooms should be a little bit more like a real room. In the rec room (living room), the chairs should face the TV, not away from it. (For the next game update.)

## Items and puzzles

- **Getting Jon out (scene 1, spaceship, playing as May)** — objects nearby that May can use to entice Jon out of his hideout (which objects: not decided yet).
- **Engine room key** — inside a can of tuna in the refrigerator (spaceship kitchen) → opens the engine room. To open the tuna can, May needs the can opener, which is in the kitchen drawers. Also in those drawers: a knife, a soup scoop, a corkscrew, a bottle opener, some aluminum foil, and a whisk. Puzzle: search through the different items in the refrigerator. There should be several other items, and the tuna needs to be checked. Other items in the refrigerator: mango, apple, moldy cheese, garlic, pickled herring, kimchi, one expired (or almost expired) milk, and one unexpired milk. (Kids also said "making everything almost expired.")
- **Moldy cheese** — from the refrigerator → give to the mouse → the mouse gives May a map of the engine room schematics, which they need to be able to go [through the engine room — kids were still talking]. The map is needed because the spaceship is stalled in space and not moving, and May needs Jon to help because he's the engineer. Jon uses these instructions to repair the engine, which is offline. The mouse is in the kitchen.
- **Olivia's memory pills** — in the engine room (looks: 11-memory-pills.png) → bring them to Olivia → then she can tell May what's going on with Jon. After the pills, Olivia remembers that Jon likes chocolate cake a lot.
- **Chocolate cake** — they have to make a chocolate cake for Jon → give it to Jon → May uses it to convince him to come out and fix the ship. Ingredients: flour, oil, sugar, a little bit of water, some yeast, and a lot of cacao. The ingredients are spread all over the spaceship: one item in each of the people's rooms. Where each one is (kids picked option 1, which I suggested when they said "you decide"): May's room: sugar. Olivia's room: flour. Jasin's room: oil. Jon's room: cacao. Bathroom: water. Storage: yeast.
- **Space flower** — on the windowsill in the kitchen of the ship → the kids bring it to Woody in the scary forest ([07-scary-forest.md](07-scary-forest.md)) → he helps move the sleeping trees off the road. The kids can grab it at the beginning, if they're really smart. If not, they go back to the ship for it later, after Woody says, "I wish I had a space flower." Jasin is the one who remembers it. To get back to the ship (it's in the water), they have to steal the boat from the apprentice of the witch, by going to the swamp on foot.
- **Armory keycard** (from Dandy and the kids, told to the game developer session) — the key is in the portal (Jon's portable hole), underneath a pile of papers → it opens a locked box in engineering (the engine room) → inside is the keycard → it opens the armory door in the control room. (Dictated as "a blocked box and engineering" and "pilot papers." Need to check: "a pile of papers" or "pilot papers"?)
