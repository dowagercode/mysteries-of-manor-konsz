# 5. The road map

- After the gas station, they don't go straight to the swamp. In between, there's a **road map**: you see the jeep as a miniature on the map, and it looks kind of like the world map from Super Mario World. The map shows the places they've already been, and then they can continue. Stops on the map, in order: (1) a **closed supermarket**, but it's locked; (2) a **scary forest**; (3) the **swamp**.
- **The road map** — after the gas station. The jeep drives on the map as a miniature, like the Super Mario World map. Shows places already visited.
- Later in the story, after the dogs of darkness, the witch's house (with the wizard and the witch inside) moves around the map.
