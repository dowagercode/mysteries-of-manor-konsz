# 2. The crash site (the beach)

- Asteroids make the spaceship fall from space into the water. The kids yell "Ahhh!" and then say "Shucks" because they're upset they fell into the water (05-spaceship-splashdown.png).
- **Zone: the crash site** — where they land, on the beach. (First zone.)
- When they land on the beach, Jasin's ball and Olivia's tutu start glowing. They jump in a startled way, and wonder what could have caused it.
- **Cutscene from the beach:** it shows the castle in the distance, behind the hills and forests, with lightning crashing all around it. Jon says, "I wonder if there's a wizard there who can tell us about this?" (This is how the kids decide to go to the wizard.)
