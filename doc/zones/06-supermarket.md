# 6. The closed supermarket

First stop on the road map.

## Place

- **Zone: the closed supermarket** — first stop on the road map. It's locked. The kids find a way inside with a key from the gas station restroom. Inside: piles and piles of ramen, and then just one mac and cheese. (Dictation first heard "one skis"; then the kids said "mac and cheese.") The problem: buried under the pile of everything, which you only see if you move away the mac and cheese box, is the lady who is the shopkeeper. She's been buried by the ramen. Why: the ramen fell during an earthquake that was caused by one of the wizard's experiments. What fixes it: the kids get her out by levitating the ramen with telekinesis (May's power). After they rescue her, the shopkeeper lady tells them how to go to the swamp, and to avoid the witch.

## Items and puzzles

- **Rescuing the shopkeeper** — closed supermarket → move the mac and cheese box to find her under the ramen → levitate the ramen with telekinesis → she's free and tells them how to go to the swamp and to avoid the witch. (The ramen fell in an earthquake caused by one of the wizard's experiments.)
- **Straw hat** — underneath the rubble of the store, found when the shopkeeper lady is freed from being under there → used to make the giant scarecrow at the swamp ([08-swamp-and-witch.md](08-swamp-and-witch.md)).
