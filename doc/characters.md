# Characters

The four kids. (The bad guys are in [bad-guys.md](bad-guys.md).)

- **May** — hair in two round buns, sparkly eyes, a dress. Her favorite thing is probably dancing. Her special thing is probably that whenever she spins, her friends float (earlier the kids said "maybe both" float and glow). "It's kind of just fun to watch." ADDED: May has telekinesis, to some abilities. She can lift most things, but if it's up to 20,000 pounds, she has to try extremely hard. There's a kind of increase of effort for how heavy something is.
- **Jon** — spiky hair, star shirt, shorts. He's the engineer on the spaceship. His tall purple oval opens up. When he puts it on the ground, it turns into a room he can hide in. He takes it everywhere he goes.
- **Jasin** — short messy hair, big smile. Carries a soccer ball. He loves soccer; it's his favorite thing ever in his life. The ball is magic: when he kicks it, it glows, and he doesn't know why. The thing under his arm is another soccer ball. He loves soccer, so he wants a bunch of balls.
- **Olivia** — a girl ("she"). She's a ballerina. Sparkly eyes. Hair changed to pigtails (it was long hair in the drawing). The purple thing at her waist is really a tutu, not a disc. (Drawing spells her name "Oliver.") Her tutu isn't really magic, but sometimes when she spins, the glowing of her tutu gets really strong and it makes her friend float. They want to figure out why that happens to her tutu.
