# Mysteries of Manor Konsz — Design Notes

Title (decided by the kids): **Mysteries of Manor Konsz** — "Mysteries" (plural), spelled "Konsz." (The cover drawing says "Konss.")

Point-and-click story game with magical mysteries (like "Mystery of Woolie Mountain"): clickable scenes, item drawer, using items on things, talking to characters.

Designers: Dandy, Addie, and a teammate.

## Characters

- **May** — hair in two round buns, sparkly eyes, a dress. Her favorite thing is probably dancing. Her special thing is probably that whenever she spins, her friends float (earlier the kids said "maybe both" float and glow). "It's kind of just fun to watch." ADDED: May has telekinesis, to some abilities. She can lift most things, but if it's up to 20,000 pounds, she has to try extremely hard. There's a kind of increase of effort for how heavy something is.
- **Jon** — spiky hair, star shirt, shorts. He's the engineer on the spaceship. His tall purple oval opens up. When he puts it on the ground, it turns into a room he can hide in. He takes it everywhere he goes.
- **Jasin** — short messy hair, big smile. Carries a soccer ball. He loves soccer; it's his favorite thing ever in his life. The ball is magic: when he kicks it, it glows, and he doesn't know why. The thing under his arm is another soccer ball. He loves soccer, so he wants a bunch of balls.
- **Olivia** — a girl ("she"). She's a ballerina. Sparkly eyes. Hair changed to pigtails (it was long hair in the drawing). The purple thing at her waist is really a tutu, not a disc. (Drawing spells her name "Oliver.") Her tutu isn't really magic, but sometimes when she spins, the glowing of her tutu gets really strong and it makes her friend float. They want to figure out why that happens to her tutu.

## Who the player controls

- CHANGED: You don't choose your character. Instead, the character changes based on the scene: you play each scene as one of the kids, as they take place in the story, and the protagonist for that scene is chosen for you.
- (Earlier idea, now replaced: choose a kid at the start, and pick a different kid when you play again.)
- Gameplay feel: point-and-click where you move your character around, like Day of the Tentacle or Mystery of Woolie Mountain.
- Structure idea (Dandy): the game is made in chapters, like Kentucky Route Zero.
- Art style idea (Dandy, still exploring): characters and settings as low-res 3D models, like early PlayStation or N64.
- Voices: each character has their own voice (what each one sounds like: not decided yet). Dandy: the voices are stilted and overlap each other; they should speak a little slower and not overlap or cut each other off.
- Abilities are never the automatic first action (Dandy): for example, when May clicks an object, she shouldn't try to float it first. Instead, give an option to float it if the player wants to. This applies everywhere, not just the ping-pong scene.
- Ping-pong scene: you can click on each character and have them do their special move. The special move should not happen by default when you select a character (you choose to use it). Special moves in the ping-pong fight: Olivia spins and floats her friends. Jasin kicks the soccer ball, and it glows and can be used as a fireball. Jon has his portal, so he can hide everyone inside to protect them. May has telekinesis and can throw objects or people around.
- How they beat the ping-pong bad guy (kids started from Plan 3, "Team Combo," from options I offered, then changed the order): 1. The ping-pong guy attacks the first time → Jon hides everyone in his portal. 2. Before Olivia comes out → Jasin kicks his fireball. 3. The ping-pong guy attacks again → Olivia spins and lifts everyone up, and May grabs the stick with her mind and bonks him. He's knocked out.
- Witch scene: no single kid. Where you point and click, the whole group moves together.
- Scene 1 (spaceship): you play as May. She has to figure out how to get Jon out (of his hideout). There will be a little mystery around that, with some objects nearby that she can use to entice him out (objects not decided yet). May has to go and talk to some of the other characters to find out exactly what Jon needs to come out, because he's actually trying to hide from someone. Jon knows who, but he's not going to tell anyone. In his mind he's saying, "It's the bad guys." He heard on the iPad in the room that there are bad guys, so he's hiding in a secret room.
- Scene 1 rules: the game should not move to the next part of the story until the first part is resolved. May goes around the ship and asks other crew members what's going on. The whole ship can be explored, with rooms inside to explore (Crew: Olivia, May, Jon, and Jasin.)
- Spaceship rooms: 4 bedrooms, one kitchen, one living room, one bathroom, one playroom, the cockpit ("it's a spaceship, so they need the cockpit"), a control room area, an engine room, and storage. Each room needs a door, and there's a hallway that connects them all. Jon's hideout is in Jon's room: it's a portable hole, so it doesn't need its own door in the hallway. May can go inside it too, to talk to him.
- Ship layout (Dandy): the hallway is in the center of all the rooms, like a real ship, with the cabins on the sides of a central hallway. The engineering room is at the rear end, and the control room is at the fore (front) end. The kitchen is its own kind of zone; maybe the kitchen and the rec room are connected to each other in the center, like the hub of everything. Confirmed by Dandy: the living room is the rec room, and the control room and cockpit are one room. Order along the hallway, back to front: engine room, storage, bathroom, May's room, Jon's room, the hub (kitchen + living room joined by an open archway), Jasin's room, Olivia's room, playroom, control room.
- Ship look (Dandy): the ship looks too homogeneous right now. Each room needs its own distinctive decoration and look. Don't make it a monotonous hallway; maybe view it from a different angle. Exploring should be fun and interesting, not a chore. (What each room looks like: ask the kids.) May's room: see 12-mays-room.png (hearts, heart balloons, Barbie Dream House). Jasin's room: see 13-jasins-room.png (soccer balls everywhere).
- Ship view (Dandy picked, in the game developer session): a cutaway, like a dollhouse sliced open from the side. It replaces the long hallway and the map. Engine room at the back (left), control room at the front (right). Top deck: bathroom, Olivia's room, playroom. Middle deck: kitchen + living room hub, Jasin's room. Bottom deck: May's room, Jon's room, storage. May climbs ladders between decks. Room decorations are placeholders until the kids describe or draw their rooms.
- Clue style (picked from options I offered): Dollhouse View + Follow the Mouse. The kitchen mouse peeks out in different rooms, leaving little footprints or crumbs that lead toward the next clue, and kids follow the trail around the ship.
- Camera and exits (Dandy, after trying it): show the exit on screen. Move away from the side view. Have doors on both sides. Use more of a cinematic angle that shifts with the different locations you go to.
- When May asks Olivia what's going on with Jon, Olivia says: "I don't know. I can't tell you unless you bring me my memory pills."
- To find Olivia's memory pills, you have to go into the engine room, but the key to the engine room is inside the refrigerator, in a can of tuna.
- The spaceship is stalled in space and not moving. May needs Jon to help, because he's the engineer. (That's why she needs the engine room map.)
- When May brings Olivia her memory pills, Olivia remembers that Jon likes chocolate cake a lot. So they have to make a chocolate cake for Jon to get him to come out of the hole.
- May uses the chocolate cake to convince Jon to come out and fix the ship.
- To fix the ship, Jon needs the instructions the mouse has (the engine room schematics). He uses them to repair the engine, which is offline.
- After Jon fixes the engine, they get hit by asteroids (which make the spaceship fall into the water).

## Story setup

- The story begins on a spaceship: the kids are on their giant spaceship in space (not a cruise ship).
- Asteroids make the spaceship fall from space into the water. The kids yell "Ahhh!" and then say "Shucks" because they're upset they fell into the water (05-spaceship-splashdown.png).
- After they swim, they run to a mountain, and then they meet the ping-pong bad guy (at the creepy ping-pong station in the middle of the mountain).
- The ping-pong bad guy keeps trying to hit them, but he can't, because when Olivia spins around, all of her friends keep on floating, and he can't hit them.
- The floating kids are getting tired of the fight, so they take his ping-pong stick and hit him with it themselves.
- He is knocked out, so they can get away now.
- Next, they go to find the witch.
- When the kids walk away, the witch secretly, so secret, uses her wand. She waves it and tries to get the kids back over and trap them in her swamp. But when Jasin hears it, he kicks his soccer ball right into the witch's guts.
- Jasin kicked so hard, the witch fell down.
- They take her to the house and lock her in a cage.
- They get into the jeep and drive to the castle.
- When they arrive at the castle, it's a very vast, barren landscape up in the mountain. There's a lot of space and distance between where they can drive to and the castle. It has a gigantic front door, but it's closed and very far away. They have to go through very dangerous rocks to climb to the door.
- The kids must get through the rocks by understanding the correct sequence of moves. There will be a kind of pattern, but it will not be apparent at first. They have to mimic a pattern in the clouds, which keeps on changing for what's needed.
- At the gigantic front door: they disguise themselves as priests from the witch's ministry and are trying to convince the wizard to join them.
- Still deciding how they get from the spaceship to the time when they have to choose their vehicles. There are a lot of other things in the story that haven't been decided yet.
- The kids want to go to the wizard to find out why Jasin's ball glows.
- The wizard is secretly a bad guy, but the kids don't know.

## The wizard

- Looks (08-wizard.png): flat purple hat, curly grey hair, big nose, smile, long grey beard, huge purple robe with wide jagged sleeves.
- He's from Los Angeles, where he was already pretty sneaky.
- When he hears the kids are coming to Manor Konsz, he goes there too. Now he's even more sneaky, and he's ready to defeat the kids.
- The kids are trying to defeat the wizard.

## The witch

- Another bad guy. (Looks: see 06-witch.png.)
- She's on the bad guy team: all of the bad guys work as a team together (with the wizard).
- She lives in a swamp near the entrance of the wizard castle.
- She has a wand. She secretly tries to trap the kids in her swamp with it.

## The ping-pong bad guy

- A ping-pong bad guy drinking beer in his left hand (07-ping-pong-bad-guy.png). On the bad guy team.
- He hits people with his ping-pong stick, and he drinks beer.
- The kids meet him at the creepy ping-pong station, in the middle of a mountain.

## The apprentice

- Drawn at the witch's house in the swamp, in a boat because the swamp has water in it (10-witchs-house.png).
- He starts as bad, but he'll become good.
- Eventually, in the story, he finds sympathy with the kids and aligns more with them.

## Places

- World size: the world should seem a little bit larger. The rooms should have some connecting areas. The way to the witch's house is full of a few different zones, and the witch's house is on chicken feet in the middle. The wizard's place also has a lot of different areas you have to go through. Each area can have puzzles (not decided yet — to write together).
- **Zone: the crash site** — where they land, on the beach. (First zone.)
- **Zone: the gas station** — further inland from the beach; this is where they find the vehicles. A very surreal place. There's one person working, and it's kind of empty. There aren't many good things to eat; it's all a little bit old. There are 3 cars parked there, and one of them is the jeep. The gas station attendant is a kind of skeleton guy who doesn't ever speak, but just kind of smiles.
- **Manor Konsz** — a giant place, very creepy.
  - **The secret mansion** — very creepy, 8 miles away. They take the creepy jeep.
    - How to get there: Olivia gives ideas (jeep, bike, train). The kids in the story each pick: May says the bike. Jon says the bike. Jasin says the train. Olivia says the jeep, because they're already going to use the jeep to get to the castle. They all agreed with Olivia: the jeep, because they were already going to take it, and using two types would probably take longer.
    - First room they see: the kitchen. Second room they see: the living room. The player chooses: words on the screen say "left" or "right." Left goes to the living room, right goes to the kitchen.
    - Kitchen: there are lots of food.
    - Living room: a couch. The couch has spiders in it and it's ripped. The couches aren't really that comfortable. A TV, but for some reason, whenever they turn it on, it's really hard to put stuff on there. A pet dog, but it's really mean. It jumps up at them, trying to bite them. All the kids run to the kitchen, running from the spiders and all the creepy stuff they see in the living room.
  - **The secret castle** — separate from the mansion, 50 miles away but still in Manor Konsz. The wizard is there. You get there in a creepy jeep. Looks (09-wizard-castle.png, "Wizard castle"): twisty claw-like towers, flame-shaped spires, a purple flag on the tallest tower, a round window with a cross, lots of small windows, an arched door with a winding path, grey sky, crescent moon.
    - Arriving: a very vast, barren landscape up in the mountain, with a lot of distance between where you can drive to and the castle. A gigantic front door that's closed and very far away. Very dangerous rocks to climb to get to the door.
    - The witch's swamp: near the entrance of the wizard castle. The witch lives there. Her house stands on big bird legs in the swamp, with a little tower and a smoking chimney (10-witchs-house.png).

- **The creepy ping-pong station** — in the middle of a mountain. The ping-pong bad guy is there.

## Items and puzzles

- **Ping-pong stick** — the ping-pong bad guy's paddle → the kids take it and hit him with it → he's knocked out and they get away.
- **Dangerous rocks puzzle** — at the wizard castle, between where they park and the gigantic front door → get through by understanding the correct sequence of moves. There's a kind of pattern, but it's not apparent at first. They figure it out by mimicking a pattern in the clouds, which keeps on changing for what's needed.
- **Priest disguises** — the kids disguise themselves as priests from the witch's ministry → at the castle's gigantic front door → to try to convince the wizard to join them. They get the disguises from exploring around the witch's house.
- **Getting Jon out (scene 1, spaceship, playing as May)** — objects nearby that May can use to entice Jon out of his hideout (which objects: not decided yet).
- **Engine room key** — inside a can of tuna in the refrigerator (spaceship kitchen) → opens the engine room. To open the tuna can, May needs the can opener, which is in the kitchen drawers. Also in those drawers: a knife, a soup scoop, a corkscrew, a bottle opener, some aluminum foil, and a whisk. Puzzle: search through the different items in the refrigerator. There should be several other items, and the tuna needs to be checked. Other items in the refrigerator: mango, apple, moldy cheese, garlic, pickled herring, kimchi, one expired (or almost expired) milk, and one unexpired milk. (Kids also said "making everything almost expired.")
- **Moldy cheese** — from the refrigerator → give to the mouse → the mouse gives May a map of the engine room schematics, which they need to be able to go [through the engine room — kids were still talking]. The map is needed because the spaceship is stalled in space and not moving, and May needs Jon to help because he's the engineer. Jon uses these instructions to repair the engine, which is offline. The mouse is in the kitchen.
- **Olivia's memory pills** — in the engine room (looks: 11-memory-pills.png) → bring them to Olivia → then she can tell May what's going on with Jon. After the pills, Olivia remembers that Jon likes chocolate cake a lot.
- **Chocolate cake** — they have to make a chocolate cake for Jon → give it to Jon → May uses it to convince him to come out and fix the ship. Ingredients: flour, oil, sugar, a little bit of water, some yeast, and a lot of cacao. The ingredients are spread all over the spaceship: one item in each of the people's rooms. Where each one is (kids picked option 1, which I suggested when they said "you decide"): May's room: sugar. Olivia's room: flour. Jasin's room: oil. Jon's room: cacao. Bathroom: water. Storage: yeast.
