Chapter 4 · The wizard's castle

50 miles away, still in Manor Konsz

The barren mountain

Dangerous rocks

A vast, empty landscape. The gigantic front door is closed and far away. The kids cross the dangerous rocks by copying a pattern in the clouds, which keeps changing.

Filled in by the game: three rows of three rocks; the clouds puff up in order.

Gigantic front door

The disguise

The kids dress as priests from the witch's ministry and try to convince the wizard to join them.

"Finally, she noticed me!" He invites them in for lemonade and muffins. When the kids ask about the glowing, he asks how the witch likes her coffee (she only drinks tea) and disappears.

Then a spiral staircase goes up to the level with the eight-room hall.

Other way in: the truck entrance behind the castle (kept for now) goes to the kitchen, which connects to the main reception area where they have tea.

Wheel-of-the-year hall

Maze · 8 rooms

Eight rooms named for the festivals, out of order. Enter them in wheel order (Samhain, Yule, Imbolc, Ostara, Beltane, Litha, Lughnasadh, Mabon) to raise a staircase in the center room. Going in the wrong order does nothing.

Hint
The statue's riddle, and a wheel chart under a cloth in a closet in the Samhain room.

Upper level · the sphinx

Riddle

"I'm buried in the ground, but I'm not treasure. Give me water and sun, and I'll grow."

Answer: SEED. One letter is hidden in each of four downstairs rooms: Yule, Ostara, Litha, Mabon.

Check with the kids: which letter goes in which room (Story Keeper suggested S, E, E, D in wheel order).

Hall of mirrors

Three hidden rooms
Past
The kids turn into toddlers among giant furniture. Move a chair, stand on a book, reach the cookie on the table, eat it: the trumpet's mouthpiece (kids to confirm).
Present
Normal size, but foggy: use the flashlight. A giant tree with an owl and its riddle: "The more you take, the more you leave behind." Answer: footsteps. Reward: the middle section.
Future
Dark: use the flashlight. Statues of the kids as grown-ups. Put each kid's stone item on the right statue (May: hair bow · Jon: portal disk · Jasin: soccer ball · Olivia: tutu). Reward: the bell.

The atrium

The wizard

Put the crystal trumpet together and blow it: the hall of mirrors shatters. The kids are in a large atrium, and the wizard is there, laughing at them.

The kids ask him about the glowing mysteries. He says their things are glowing because they should give them to him. The kids refuse, and he starts the fight:

"You little twerps, you think you can fight us? Try to defeat these dogs of darkness."

Then
He sets the dogs of darkness (3 vicious hounds) on the kids and vanishes to join the witch in her house.
Dogs
The kids throw them a can of dog food from the mansion's kitchen. Or they throw the glowing ball: the dogs chase it, opening the way to the emergency exit (a slide down and out to the outside again).
Connect: getting to the end
  • Cutscene: the wizard goes to the witch and lets her out of the cage, the house starts moving, then back to the kids. Clues to find it: chicken feathers where it was last, and wind blowing at the place it will be next (go out to the map and back in to see it). It goes to all the places on the map, and the kids must catch it 3 times to finally get to it. The first two times, it runs away and leaves feathers. The third time, it ends at the mansion exterior. The kids climb inside, but the wizard and the witch run out into the mansion, and the kids must find them in a maze there.
  • The kids marry the wizard and the witch (in the ministry robes), and they stop fighting. The things glow because the kids were chosen by the great earth spirit to bring joy and kindness to the world. At the end, the wizard, the witch, and the apprentice help fix the ship, and the kids fly on to Woolley Mountain.

Connections the story still needs

  1. The beginning: why the kids are on the ship, where they're headed, and the first strange thing they notice.
  2. The glowing mystery: answered. The great earth spirit chose the kids to bring joy and kindness to the world.
  3. Jon's bad guys: connect the bad guys from the iPad to the wizard's team (the ping-pong bad guy, the witch, the wizard).
  4. The armory: place it on the ship, name the weapons, and make sure the flashlight is always reachable before the castle.
  5. The secret mansion: the final chase ends there, with a maze where the kids must find the wizard and the witch. The maze has music-note rooms: do, re, mi, fa, so, la, ti, do. Each room plays its note when the kids enter. Play "Twinkle Twinkle Little Star" (do do so so la la so, fa fa mi mi re re do) to find the wizard and the witch. Hint: a map in the ti room shows the notes for the song. Then a staircase comes down from the ceiling up to the wizard and the witch. Wearing the ministry robes, the kids offer to marry them. They're happy and stop fighting the kids.
  6. The apprentice: after the dogs, the right compliment makes him join the kids. He helps search for the chicken house and gives the hint about the marriage.
  7. The witch's ministry: what it is, and why the wizard would want to join.
  8. The dogs of darkness: beaten with a can of dog food from the mansion's kitchen, or by throwing the glowing ball so the dogs chase it and the kids escape down the emergency-exit slide.
  9. The final showdown: the wizard frees the witch and they go mobile with the house. The kids follow clues (chicken feathers, then wind) to find it. What happens there?
  10. The ending: the kids marry the wizard and the witch, who stop fighting. The wizard and witch were grumpy because they were lonely; now they're happy. The wizard, the witch, and the apprentice help fix the ship, and the kids fly on to Woolley Mountain.
